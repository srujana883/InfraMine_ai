## 3. Data Classification and Provenance Integrity

### A. DIRECTLY DOCUMENTED VALUES
* **Quarry Distances:** Lead distances from port site explicitly published in DPR Table 2-2, Table 2-3, and Road DPR Table 1-6 (Gargaon 17 km, Khanivade 19 km, Mahagaon 20 km, Nanivali 25 km, Borsheti 24 km, Kirat 25 km, Nagzari 26 km, Girnoli 28 km).
* **Quarry Extent & Yields:** Hillock heights, projected areas (in hectares and square meters), and volumetric estimates (in traditional brass units) explicitly tabulated in Road DPR Tables 1-6 and 7-2.
* **Proved Reserves for Gargaon & Khanivade:** Gargaon Patch-1 (18.04 MMT proved mineable reserves) and Khanivade Patch-5 (12.93 MMT proved mineable reserves) calculated via four geological cross-sections (tonnage factor 2.9) in dedicated statutory EIA executive summaries.
* **Geotechnical Borehole Evidence:** Field test borehole coordinates fixed on ground by SGS Surveys Private Limited (Table 7 of Geotechnical Investigation Report) for BH-01 at Gargaon (`19.511019°N, 72.492284°E`) and BH-03 at Khanivade (`19.505854°N, 72.493684°E`) are preserved in evidence text and notes. Because these represent sub-surface geotechnical borehole drill points rather than confirmed quarry centroids or operational gateways, they are not treated as source coordinates in `latitude`/`longitude`.
* **Statutory Sand Reservation Polygon:** Exact 20 boundary points (A to T) with arcsecond latitude/longitude published by Ministry of Mines under S.O. 5411(E) reserving 10,277.10 Ha offshore of Daman (recorded in notes; centroid/gateway not fabricated).
* **Capital Dredging Volume:** 6.9 to 7.0 million m³ suitable for Phase 1 fill published in DPR Sections 8.3, 8.4, and 9.2.3.
* **Precast Concrete Armor Units:** Accropode II requirements explicitly detailed in Package 1A EPC Breakwater tender (54,000 units of 11 m³, 2,700 units of 13 m³).

### B. MISSING VALUES (LEFT AS NULL — ZERO FABRICATION)
* **Exact Quarry Gateway / Centroid GPS Coordinates:** The DPR and Road Connectivity DPR identify villages, hillock elevations, and road lead distances, but do not publish confirmed quarry entrance coordinates or centroids. Geotechnical borehole points (BH-01, BH-03) represent drill points only and are excluded from source coordinates. In strict accordance with zero-fabrication rules, `latitude` and `longitude` are recorded as `null` for all quarries. Agent 2 uses the directly documented lead distances (e.g., 17 km, 19 km) with `distance_source = 'dataset'`.
* **Specific Capacities for Steel, Bitumen, and Cement Suppliers:** Road DPR Section 7.1.2 notes these commodities are "available at Mumbai" (~100 km) or "available at Vadhavan", but does not publish individual plant capacities or warehouse quantities. `capacity_or_quantity` is recorded as `null`.
* **Exact Road Kilometers for Cement:** Road DPR Table 7-1 specifies "Available at Vadhavan" without a numeric highway kilometer distance. `distance_km` is recorded as `null`.

### C. SOURCES REQUIRING CURRENT VERIFICATION
1. **Gargaon Stone Quarry Patch-1 (`VAD-Q01`):** Requires verification of Forest Clearance Stage-I approval from MoEFCC and Environmental Clearance from SEIAA Maharashtra.
2. **Khanivade Stone Quarry Patch-5 (`VAD-Q03`):** Requires verification of Forest Clearance Stage-I approval from MoEFCC and Environmental Clearance from SEIAA Maharashtra.
3. **Private Quarries (`VAD-Q08` to `VAD-Q11` — Borsheti, Kirat, Nagzari, Girnoli):** Identified as private commercial quarries serving regional roads and buildings. Their current operating status, permit validity, commercial availability, and remaining lease life require ground confirmation prior to contractor engagement.

### D. CONFLICTING OR OVERLAPPING DOCUMENTATION NOTED
* **Nagzari Distance Variance:** Road DPR Table 7-3 and DPR Table 2-3 record the lead distance from port to Nagzari as **26 km**. However, the Techno-Economic Feasibility Report (TEFR, Section 7.3.4.1, p68) notes: *"Existing quarry sites are located at Nagzari Village which is around 35km away from the proposed port location."* Both references are documented in the provenance log.
* **Gargaon Lead Distance:** DPR Table 2-2 reports 17 km via Shigaon road, while the 2026 Gargaon EIA Summary reports 17.2 km north-west of lease centroid. The documented variance is minor ($\pm 0.2$ km).
